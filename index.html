About Us

Welcome to Old Velvet Cafe

Located near Bose Park in Bhagalpur, Old Velvet Cafe is a warm rooftop destination where premium coffee, fresh bakery treats, and unforgettable moments come together.

Whether you're catching up with friends, enjoying a peaceful evening, or working over your favorite latte, our cozy ambience is designed to make every visit memorable.
