About Old Velvet Cafe

Coffee, conversations & cozy rooftop moments.

Welcome

More Than Just A Coffee Cafe

Welcome to Old Velvet Cafe, a cozy rooftop coffee destination in the heart of Bhagalpur.

We bring together handcrafted coffee, delicious bakery treats, comforting food and a warm atmosphere where people can slow down and enjoy the moment.

Whether you're meeting friends, attending a casual meeting, working remotely or simply looking for a peaceful place to enjoy your favorite drink, Old Velvet Cafe is a place to relax and connect.

The Experience

What Makes Us Special

A cozy rooftop atmosphere, good food and great conversations come together at Old Velvet Cafe.

Handcrafted Coffee

From espresso and cappuccino to refreshing cold coffee, there's something for every coffee lover.

Fresh Bakery

Enjoy delicious bakery treats including cheesecake, brownies, doughnuts and cupcakes.

Rooftop Ambience

Unwind in a warm rooftop setting surrounded by comfortable seating and a relaxing atmosphere.

Good Vibes

Enjoy conversations, music and memorable evenings with friends and loved ones.

Our Philosophy

Slow Down. Sip. Enjoy.

We believe that a good café is more than just a place to grab a drink.

It's a place where conversations last a little longer, work feels a little easier, and an ordinary evening can turn into a memorable one.

That's the experience we want to create at Old Velvet Cafe.
